Beyond the phases

What not to over-focus on

Hand-writing code as a training goal

Build reading fluency and conceptual depth; let agents type. The exception is short, deliberate interview preparation.

Chasing every new agent tool

A new multiplexer, IDE or agent appears most weeks. Adopt one per category, re-evaluate quarterly, and invest the rest of your attention in the patterns. Tool fluency is table stakes; judgment is the differentiator.

Deep ML research before systems competence

You need enough ML to reason about model behaviour and inference, but this roadmap targets platform engineering. Do not postpone production systems until you can train a transformer from scratch.

Framework collecting

Do not build one tutorial in every agent framework. Pick one or two after you have seen the underlying loop, and ask which concepts would survive the framework disappearing next year. Be wary of frameworks with heavy hidden prompting (CrewAI is the usual example); they are quick to demo and hard to debug.

Prompt engineering as a standalone discipline

Prompts matter, but reliable systems depend on retrieval, tool contracts, policy, structured output, evaluation and observability. Treat prompt changes like code changes: version, evaluate, roll out safely.

Certifications without operating experience

An AWS certification can structure study but should come second to deploying and operating the architecture.

Kubernetes too early

Neither target role asks for it. Use the simplest runtime that fits the workload.
