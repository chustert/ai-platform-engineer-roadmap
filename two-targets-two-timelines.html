Two targets, two timelines

The two postings behind this roadmap are not the same job, and treating them as one hides a useful shortcut.

AI-native product engineerStaff AI Platform Engineer
Typical employerAI-first product company or studio shipping agents and workflow automation to clientsEstablished software company building internal agent platforms and developer tooling
What they screen forEvidence you build without being asked; a shipped product people use; heavy, critical use of AI tools; customer focus.8+ years, technical leadership in platform/infra/DevEx, production agentic experience, deep C# or Python, strong AWS.
How code gets written"Models write all of the code we ship." You drive agents and own what you merge.Drive org-wide adoption of Claude Code and custom MCP; build agent platforms others use.
Realistic apply pointAfter Phase 1–3 (around month 3–4), with a shipped AI product as the headline evidence.After Phases 1–11 plus visible leadership evidence (months 9–12), and subject to the tenure bar.

Apply to the first kind of role early. Working inside an AI-native team is itself the fastest way to earn the "production agentic experience" the Staff role demands. The capability gap to Staff closes faster than the credential gap: the Staff posting also filters on years and on having led teams, and no curriculum manufactures those. What this roadmap can do is make the leadership evidence visible – adoption you drove, standards you wrote, incidents you ran – so that when you meet the tenure bar the rest is already proven.
