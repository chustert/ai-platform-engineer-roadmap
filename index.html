The AI Platform Engineer Roadmap

A build-to-Staff curriculum for production agentic systems.

Coding agents write most of the code now. This roadmap trains what is left: directing them, verifying what they produce, and running AI systems that are reliable, economical, observable and safe.

  • 13 phases
  • 12 months
  • ~9 hours a week
  • 1 real product + 1 capstone
