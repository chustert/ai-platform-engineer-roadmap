The AI Platform Engineer Roadmap

A 12-month curriculum for engineers who build production AI systems with coding agents.

Coding agents now write most of the code. This roadmap teaches the rest of the job. You learn to direct agents, check their work, and run AI systems that are reliable, cheap to run, observable, and safe.

  • 13 phases
  • 12 months
  • about 9 hours a week
  • 1 real product and 1 capstone
Start Phase 1Why you read more code than you write

  13. 13Build Forge, the capstone platformmonths 9 to 12
