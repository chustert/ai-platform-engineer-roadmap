Apply to AI-native product roles first

The two job postings behind this roadmap describe different jobs. The table compares them.

AI-native product engineerStaff AI platform engineer
Typical employerA company or studio that builds agents and workflow automation for clients.An established software company that builds agent platforms and developer tools for its own engineers.
What they screen forThings you built without being asked, a product that people use, heavy use of AI tools, and attention to the customer.At least 8 years of experience, technical leadership in platform or developer-experience work, agents in production, deep C# or Python, and strong AWS.
How code gets written"Models write all of the code we ship." You direct the agents and you own what you merge.You get the whole engineering organisation to adopt Claude Code and internal MCP servers, and you build agent platforms that other teams use.
When you can applyAfter Phase 3, around month 3 or 4, with a shipped AI product as your main evidence.After Phase 11, around month 9 to 12, with visible leadership work. The tenure requirement still applies.

Apply for the product roles early. A job on an AI-native team gives you production experience with agents faster than any side project, and the Staff role asks for exactly that experience.

The Staff posting also asks for years of experience and for time spent leading teams. No curriculum can give you those. What the roadmap can do is make your leadership work visible: tools you got teams to adopt, standards you wrote, and incidents you ran. When you meet the tenure requirement, the rest of your case is already made.
